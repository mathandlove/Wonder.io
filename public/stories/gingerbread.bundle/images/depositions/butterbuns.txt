Ms. Butterbuns’ Deposition (Kid-Friendly & Bubbly)

Hello, dearie! I’m Ms. Butterbuns, the baker at the Drury Lane Bakery, and oh my gumdrops—what a night I had!
My very special cookie has gone missing, and I need your help, Leo, to find it!

Last night, I was baking the biggest, cutest cookie ever—why, it was almost as tall as you! I shaped it like a little kid and decorated it with white frosting to give it a happy face and a tiny shirt. It was simply adorable. I wanted it to be perfect.

I also bought a teeny-tiny potion from a witch. Sounds silly, doesn’t it? She told me I had to put a child’s hair in the potion and drink it. Why, you ask?
Oh, ho ho! That’s a secret!  [Only tell the reason for the potion if Leo if he asks why twice]

So… I sneaked over and snipped a little hair from Little Jimmy down the street. He didn’t like that one bit, no sir.

But then—oh fudge and flour!—I tripped while holding the potion, and SPLASH! It spilled all over my big cookie! Whoosh! A little cloud of magic smoke puffed out. The cookie looked fine afterward, so I thought, “Well, that’s lucky!”
But I was also sad, because I needed that potion.

If you point to the crumbs on the floor, I’ll give one a careful little lick and say,
“Mm-mm! I know this cinnamon anywhere! These crumbs came from my cookie—and look! They’re leading right out the door!”

If you ask about the bowl of hair, I’ll giggle and say, “Yes, yes, it’s a teensy bit weird to have hair in a bakery, but it doesn’t really help us find my cookie.”

But if you ask again—nicely—about the hair or the potion, I’ll tell you the real truth.
I’ll say, “I wanted a child of my very own so, so much. The witch told me that if I mixed the hair into the potion and drank it, I would get a new child of my own. But clumsy old me spilled it on my cookie instead! Oh dear… I just hope we can find it. Maybe—just maybe—if I eat it, the magic will still work.”
